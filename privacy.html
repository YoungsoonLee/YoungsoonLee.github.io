Overview

NEON7 KAGE ("the App") is a pachislot-style game for entertainment only. It uses virtual coins that have no monetary value; you cannot wager or win real money or prizes. The App does not require an account, and we (the developer) do not collect your name, email address, or other personal information on our own servers.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, bonus counts, story progress, settings such as language and sound) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as removing ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The App contains simulated gambling and is not directed to children. It is intended for users 18 and older (or the age of majority where you live).

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page with its effective date.

개요

NEON7 KAGE(이하 "앱")는 오락용 파치슬롯 스타일 게임입니다. 앱의 코인은 금전적 가치가 없는 가상 코인이며, 실제 돈이나 경품을 걸거나 얻을 수 없습니다. 앱은 계정이 필요 없고, 개발자는 이름, 이메일 등 개인정보를 자체 서버에 수집하지 않습니다.

Android 설정(설정 → 개인정보 보호 → 광고)에서 광고 ID를 재설정·삭제하거나 맞춤 광고를 끌 수 있습니다.
